    Questions to ask your lender

    Ask these before you sign anything.

    1. What is the interest rate, and is it fixed or does it change?
    2. What is the processing fee, and are there any other charges?
    3. Is there a penalty if I repay early or close the loan early?
    4. What is the total amount I will repay over the whole loan?
    5. Will you report this loan to the credit bureau, and how do missed payments count?

    Honest about what this is

    Loan Coach is a hackathon prototype. It prepares you to apply. It does not approve loans.

    Our rules, in plain words

    • We use your lowest month, not your best one, because a loan has to survive a bad month.
    • Total EMIs should stay within 40% of that lowest month. This is a common rule of thumb. Real lenders set their own limit.
    • The EMI uses the standard formula with the interest rate you set (18% by default).
    • Lenders like regular earning, so we look for at least 75% of weeks earned (20 of 26).
    • The readiness score is out of 100: 40 points for steady weeks, 40 for how much room your EMIs leave, and 20 for how little your income swings.
    • Every result shows the rule behind it. Nothing is hidden.

    Questions you may have

    Is Loan Coach a bank or a consultant?

    Neither. It is a free tool. It does not lend money, take fees or give legal advice.

    Is my data stored?

    No. Everything you type stays in your browser while the page is open. There is no account and no database.

    Why not just ask a bank?

    A bank decides after you apply. Loan Coach helps you prepare before you apply, so a rejection does not catch you by surprise.

    What would a real version need?

    Consented bank and UPI data through India's Account Aggregator system, and income confirmed by the platforms people earn on. This prototype uses numbers you type.
